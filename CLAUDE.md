# The City Heroes (גיבורי העיר)

Renamed 2026-11-17 to **The City Heroes** (Liora bought thecityheroes.com): English comic logo (Bangers font), page title and app name.

Liora's side project (started 2026-11-09): a learning game for **Bar and Yarden, two boys
aged 5**. Goal: practice letters, numbers, basic reading, basic writing and basic math.
Liora is a beginner with no programming experience: explain simply, in Hebrew.

## How it works
- Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build, no install: double-click `index.html` (works from file://).
  Scripts are plain globals (no ES modules, they fail on file://). Desktop shortcut
  "גיבורי העיר" opens it in Edge with `icon.ico` (rendered from `icon.svg`).
- **Voice (2026-11-15):** Liora found the browser voice "very AI" (they played in Edge, so it was already Microsoft's natural voice). Chosen: pre-recorded natural AI voice, recommended **ElevenLabs** (she'll try it and get a key). Speech is said as lists of parts (fixed sentence + word/number); `Voice.say` plays `audio/clips/*.mp3` from `audio/clips.js` when every part has a clip, else falls back to the browser voice. `node tools/make-voice.js --count | --voices | --sample | (all)` makes them; key in `.env.local` (gitignored). ~414 clips, ~10.5K characters. Numbers are said in counting form (אחת, שתיים...).
- The game **speaks** (browser speechSynthesis, he-IL), because the kids can't read
  instructions. Speech text uses niqqud; on-screen text doesn't. This PC has
  "Microsoft Asaf" (Windows Hebrew voice); Edge also has natural online voices (preferred).
- Kids are boys: speech uses masculine singular ("מצא", "בחר").
- Heroes (2026-11-09, Liora's names): **בר איש האש** (red suit, flame emblem, fireball, flames behind him) and **ירדן איש הברק** (blue suit, lightning emblem and bolts). Set by `power` in `HEROES` (`js/data.js`); drawn in `js/art.js` with gradients.
- Flow: choose hero (separate progress in
  localStorage `city-heroes-v1`) → illustrated city map (`js/map.js`): the hero token walks
  along the road to the place clicked → mission intro (hero vs villain) → 5 challenges →
  reward → hero base.
- **Look (2026-11-09, Liora asked for "Marvel-style"):** an *original* comic-book superhero
  style (thick black outlines, hard shadows, halftone dots, "בּוּם!" bursts, Karantina
  display font + Rubik). No Marvel characters, names or logos. Villain: **ד״ר בלגן**
  (Dr. Balagan, original), who messes up each place.
- **21 worlds** (2026-11-09, Liora asked for at least 20, each with real content and design): `ZONES` in `js/data.js` (story, storyText, teaches = shown in the parents corner), tasks in `ZONE_CHALLENGES` (`js/challenges.js` for the first 9 worlds, `js/challenges2.js` for the 12 newer ones: circus balloons, bakery candles/ten-frame, treasure number grid/number line, under-the-sea same-word/color words, music drum syllables, lab patterns/odd one out/sorting, forest connect-the-dots letters, fire station house numbers/ladder, stadium scores, dragon memory game, color studio color-by-number/letter, ice palace before/after, dominoes, number bonds). 52 task types. Bosses: robot, dragon, villain.
- **31 worlds in 6 neighborhoods** (2026-11-15, Liora: "more screens, a lot more games, redesign the map, not one big page"): `NEIGHBORHOODS` in `js/data.js` (5 worlds each) + the fortress. `js/map.js`: `worldSVG` = big city map (island with 6 neighborhoods + fortress, one screen), `hoodSVG` = a screen per neighborhood with its 5 places; the hero token walks the roads. A neighborhood opens when every world of the previous one was played once (or one of its own worlds was already played); parents can open all (`state.settings.openAll`). 10 newer worlds + ~25 games in `js/challenges3.js` (monster, maze, farm sorting, post office/name typing, luna park position words/dice, shapes, giants sizes/scale, magic school number tracing/final letters, fishing, meteors). Connect-the-dots was removed (Liora reported a problem) and replaced by "type the missing letter". 77 game types.
- **Third hero** (2026-11-15, Liora): **גיא איש המחץ** (Guy the Smash Man), green, power "smash" (impact-star emblem, shock waves, flying rocks, punch burst; black hair; his base is a sunny day). Heroes have `spokenAnd` ("וּבַר", "וְיַרְדֵּן", "וְגַיְא") for joined names.
- **Playing together**: "משחקים ביחד!" opens a choose-players screen (`showTeamPick`, CSS class `who-plays`; note `.team-pick` is taken by the stadium game) where 2 or 3 heroes are picked (`TEAM`, remembered in `state.settings.lastTeam`). Turns rotate through `TEAM`; 6 tasks per mission (3 each for two, 2 each for three).
- **Two-player mode** (2026-11-15): "משחק זוגי" button on the home screen (`team` in `js/app.js`). Turns alternate per task (6 tasks, 3 each; who starts alternates per mission, `state.settings.nextFirst`); speech starts with "הַתּוֹר שֶׁל ...". Each kid plays at his own level, mistakes are counted per kid, and both get their own reward. The map uses both kids' progress added together (`mp()`), position in `state.settings.teamAt`; the token shows both faces; the base screen has a tab per kid.
- **Hero base** redrawn (2026-11-15, "it looks poor"): a full scene in `baseSVG` (sky by hero theme: sunset for fire, night with moon for lightning; mountains, city, hill, dome HQ, tower, rocket on pad...). Parts not yet earned show as dotted outlines (`.ghost-part`).
- **3D look** (2026-11-15, "design like a real game, not flat"): the SVG filter `#toon3d` (in `index.html`: soft light, highlight and shadow) is applied with CSS to places, neighborhoods, base parts and bosses; buttons and cards have a shine and a darker bottom edge; maps have ground texture, sunlight and vignette.
- **Tablets** (2026-11-15): maps fit the screen height; a "turn the tablet sideways" tip on maps in portrait; bigger touch targets and the task fills the screen (`@media (pointer: coarse)`); no stuck hover effects. Sound: the first touch unlocks speech, audio and effects (needed on iPad); one reused audio player for recorded clips; the best Hebrew device voice is picked (natural/enhanced/Google first) and parents can choose voice and speed (saved in localStorage `city-heroes-voice`), with tips for downloading iPad "Carmit (Enhanced)" or Android Google Hebrew voice data. Add to home screen opens like an app (`manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/`, fullscreen landscape); `sw.js` keeps a copy for playing offline (internet first, so updates arrive).
- **Three kingdoms** (2026-11-16, Liora): `REALMS` in `js/data.js` = numbers (math), letters (lang), English (en), each with neighborhoods (`NEIGHBORHOODS`, 8 in all; `theme` picks the background) and a **ruler** (zones `ruler-math` Mega-Robot, `ruler-lang` the witch Balbula, `ruler-en` Mister Mix; bosses in `BOSS_ART`, battles mix the kingdom's tasks). Maps: city (3 kingdoms + fortress) → kingdom (`realmSVG`: neighborhoods + ruler castle) → neighborhood (`hoodSVG`). In a kingdom, neighborhood 1 is open, the next opens when the previous is fully played; the ruler opens when the whole kingdom is played; the fortress opens when all 3 rulers are beaten (parents can open all).
- **English** (`js/challenges4.js`): very basic, listening-based (hear a word with an English voice → pick the picture; colors, numbers, ABC letters, animals and their sounds, body, counting in English). Speech parts can be `{ en: "apple" }`; a right answer can "teach" (`api.correct(teach)`: says the English word and the Hebrew meaning). Words in `EN_WORDS` etc.
- **Final battle**: the fortress = 2 tasks from each kingdom + `finalShowdown` (3 quick questions fire the hero's power at Dr. Balagan's flying machine). After winning: `showVictory` (fireworks, gold medal, Balagan apologizes, printable certificate), `p.saved` counts wins; the base lists rulers beaten and city medals.
- **Each hero's own rewards** (`js/themes.js`): prize names/icons by power (`REWARD_THEMES`, e.g. Bar: lava fortress, Yarden: storm tower, Guy: stone fortress); the base drawing changes per power (sky, scenery, tower, lamps, garden, roof); power effects use the hero's color.
- **Adding heroes**: ➕ card on the home screen → `showHeroMaker` (name, boy/girl for the title only, power from 8: fire, bolt, smash, ice, nature, water, wind, magic, suit and hair color). Saved in `state.custom`, registered into `HEROES` at load (`registerCustomHeroes`); deletable in the parents corner. Speech stays masculine for everyone.
- **20 worlds per kingdom** (2026-11-16, Liora): `js/data2.js` adds 24 worlds (neighborhoods `toys` for math, `stories` for letters, `beach` + `rainbow` for English; Hello Town and ABC Park grew to 5), more English words (family, home, toys, sea, weather, fruit, vehicles, clothes, bugs, feelings), `EN_SHAPES`, syllable words, opposites, picture stories, sentences, who-does-what. Games in `js/challenges5.js` (make-ten pairs, number train, towers, fair sharing, count by twos, measuring, syllable building, word-picture memory, opposites, story order, complete the sentence, who does it, English yes/no, English memory/balloons/fish per topic, big/small, shapes, color+shape, "A is for Apple"). Kingdom maps show 4 neighborhoods. 127 game types.
- **One visual language for all maps** (2026-11-16): city, kingdom and neighborhood maps share `districtHTML` (raised platform + big emoji pictures + gold ribbon + progress badge), `glowingRoad`, `MAP_DEFS` (light, vignette, grass texture), drifting clouds and birds. Every world is drawn from `PLACE_PICS` (3 emoji); neighborhoods use `HOOD_LOOK` (ground colors + scenery). Parents corner shows progress per kingdom (bars, ruler crowns) and the worlds list folded by kingdom. The base shows only the next part to earn as a dotted outline.
- **Richer game** (2026-11-17, `js/extras.js`): helpers per kingdom (`HELPERS`: Bubo robot, Hachmon owl, Polly parrot) introduce missions and give hints (💡 button: glows the right option via `el.dataset.answer` set in `choiceQuestion`, counts as a mistake); challenge screens have a themed scene behind them (`sceneHTML`, from `HOOD_LOOK`) and the hero beside the task who cheers/thinks (`heroReact`); a pet per hero by power (`PETS`, egg → baby → grown → super by missions done); sticker album (📒 in the top bar, one sticker per world, gold after 3 missions, plus special stickers); daily surprise chest (once a day per hero: 3 stars + special sticker); opening comic story (5 panels, first time, also "📖 הסיפור" on home); Dr. Balagan taunts on the city map.
- Level per place = 1 + missions done there / 3 (max 3).
- Mistakes are never punished; after 2 wrong tries the answer glows. Stars: ≤1 mistake 3★.
- Rewards cycle: 2 base parts, then a superpower (drawn on the hero), then medals.
- Typing uses key *positions* (e.code) mapped to the Hebrew layout.
- Tracing: canvas mask of the letter (Arial bold); pass at ≥62% covered, reset if >40%
  of the ink is outside.
- Words with syllable count `s` (0 = skip; words ending in patach ganuv are skipped).
- Math is shown left-to-right. Numbers use Rubik (Karantina digits are hard to read).
